We present an integrated reactive-synthesis and mixed-integer convex
programming (MICP) framework for terrain-adaptive quadrupedal locomotion,
combining correct-by-construction symbolic-level controllers with physical-
feasibility certificates. Building on our IROS 2025 paper (DOI: [TODO];
licence: [TODO]), this article additionally contributes (i) a delay-aware
coordination scheme between the strategy automaton and the tracking
controller, (ii) online kinematic re-targeting, (iii) collision-region
selection in the online MICP, and (iv) full hardware validation against
pure-MIP and heuristic baselines on two robot platforms.

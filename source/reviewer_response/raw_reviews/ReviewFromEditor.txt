submitted to the IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO) has been reviewed

On the basis of the reviewers' ratings and comments, I regret to inform
you that your paper is not suitable for publication in T-RO.

=== Begin Editorial Board Comments === 

Three experts in the domain provided insightful feedback on the
manuscript.
All reviewers raise a major concern that the current manuscript is a
direct extension of [24], published in 2025 in the IROS proceedings.
While this submission essentially presents additional hardware
experiments, it reuses the very same materials and approaches.

As mentioned in the guidelines T-RO Information for Authors
(https://www.ieee-ras.org/publications/t-ro/t-ro-information-for-author
s/):
"If your submission is based on previously published papers, your
manuscript must not be just a mere extension of the previous
version(s),
filling in, e.g., details of proofs or additional corollaries,
additional
experiments, or a more detailed background and narrative. Rather, the
new submission must contain new results of substantive research
significance and impact beyond the previous papers."

Unfortunately, in the current state, the submission does not provide
new results of substantive research significance and impact beyond the
previous papers. The editors have assessed that the potential impact of
this work needs to be more significant for a successful review process
at T-RO. 

=== End Editorial Board Comments ===

We thank you for your interest in the IEEE Transactions on Robotics,
and hope that you will consider publishing future papers here.
